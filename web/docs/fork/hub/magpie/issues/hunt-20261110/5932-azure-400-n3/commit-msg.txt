gateway: a relay's Azure resource-mismatch 400 fails over and rests the failing relay (#5932)

A relay fronting Azure OpenAI that spreads one key over several
resources answers a conversation another of its resources began with
400 "The requested item was created under a different Azure OpenAI
resource." prevForeign knows the refusal, and passthrough asks once
more with the items magpie holds taken out; but when no repair is
available (Codex sends its own full history with store:false) or the
repaired ask is refused again, retryable did not include the refusal,
so a group stopped on it: the client got the 400, no other member was
asked, the failing relay took no rest, and the next request could pick
the same relay again (clhchan: eight group requests in five minutes,
each on the failing relay, no rest record, affinity why:"back" coming
back from the healthy spare).

Now retryable counts a recognized prevForeign refusal as retryable:
the next candidate answers before any reply reaches the client, and
the failing candidate takes its normal error rest, capped by its
rest-cap as any failure. A successful repair still returns normally
and rests nobody, and an ordinary input-validation 400 still reaches
the client without failover: the words of this refusal, and no word
list retryable matches, overlap.

retryable, this classification reused whole), reworked onto the
current shape of the switch — the reporter holds the diagnosis, the

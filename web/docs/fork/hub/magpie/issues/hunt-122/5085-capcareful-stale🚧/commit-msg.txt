provider: a usage reading kept from before no one asked for again is not a failed one (#5085)

A saved Claude account's usage is read only when its card is refreshed,
when the switch off a spent account needs it, or when the keep-alive
runs (claudeWindows, #1630); any other reading of it is what was kept.
What was kept comes back through keepLast with AsOf set and
ReadDeferred set (no read was made this time), and state() counted
every reading with AsOf as a failed one. So a saved account sat in
"its usage couldn't be read" for a careful provider: capHold held it at
its cap with its own reading in hand (30% used of a 90% cap in #5085),
and the careful re-read came back as kept as it was, so nothing but a
manual card refresh ever brought it back. It was held whatever the
distance from its cap.

A kept reading no one asked for again is what the account last said,
not a failed one. It stands as its reading, dated as the rounds see it,
as a kept reading in memory already does, so a careful provider passes
it while it is under its cap and holds it by its age near one. A kept
reading shown in place of a read that failed keeps counting as failed,
as of the good one: what can't be told is still not spent
(plugins#349). TestUsageReadDeferredIsNotAFailedRead pins both — red on
the old state(), green here.

The Routing page said "past the cap" of a held account whatever held
it: leftWhy and unlistedWord now say so when the trace's unread says
the usage can't be told, in the four languages the page speaks, and the
routing subsystem doc names the distinction.

Same root as #5079, the other half of it: there the careful re-read
never reaches Claude's saved accounts; here the kept reading counted as
a failed one kept the account held even far under its cap. This touches
neither ReadUsageNow nor claudeWindows, so the two compose.

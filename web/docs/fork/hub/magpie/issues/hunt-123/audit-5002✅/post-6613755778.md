**What's wrong**

`"access_token_expires_at": "2026-08-30T11:44:31.312645+08:00"` went out as `"access_token_expires_at": "{{SECRET_…}}:44:31.312645+08:00"` — the field's name has `token` in it, and the head of the moment to its hour is all a secret's characters hold (the time's colons are none of theirs), so `2026-08-30T11` masked and the minutes went to the vendor as written. Credit @dchaofei for the one-line capture; it reproduces the shape exactly.

**Root cause**

The value-side checks (`fieldAt`, `keyAt` in `internal/redact/fieldvalue.go`) take any run of a secret's characters after a field or key whose name says secret — and an RFC 3339 timestamp's head (`2026-08-30T11`) **is** such a run: digits, dashes, a `T`, more digits. Nothing told the two apart, so a moment beside a token-ish field name masked from its start. The same head masked after `令牌是`, `密钥为`, `token 是`, `apikey 为` and beside `SERVER_KEY`, `DB_PASS` — every path into the value-side check.

**The fix** (4 files, +82/−20)

`timestampValue` in `fieldvalue.go`: a value that **is** a moment as RFC 3339 writes one — whole, or its head to the hour — goes as it came; a value that only *starts* as a moment still masks, what follows the hour being the key's. What the CJK password rule takes after `密码` it still takes, digits only. The check order in `json.go`/`redact.go` shifts by the same idea, nothing else.

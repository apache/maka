checkin: a DarkWake's failure hours later doesn't stack the offline tries

A Mac that sleeps through midnight is woken now and then by a DarkWake
too short for the check-in to finish (#5020: "The operation timed out"
in a 2-7 s wake). Each of those failures counted as one more try in a
row of them, and the backoff the row had earned, doubled to half an
hour, stood between the machine's waking for good and the day's
check-in: mufanmu's logs show the look two minutes after the lid opened
kept back by the DarkWake of half an hour before, and the day went by
without a check-in.

Tries in a row are now only so while the last try's backoff was still
running: a failure hours later is the day's first again, and the look
on waking for good asks at once. What #265's backoff is for — tries
close enough together that the network never came back between them —
is kept.

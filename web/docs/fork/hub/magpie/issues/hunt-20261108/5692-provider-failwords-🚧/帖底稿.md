An agent asking for JSON gets a JSONDecodeError, one asking for code an advert, request after request, for as long as the plan stays spent, while the Usage and Routing pages say 200 the whole time. **Root cause**: every one of the five doors a 200 with an error behind it could go through needs the reply to *look* wrong, and a legal completion with a pitch in it doesn't. `failure` is the only classifier and runs once a try has failed already (`routing.go:567`), `carriedStatus` re-reads a 2xx body but only for an `error` envelope or a word `streamStatus` knows (`routing.go:553`) — and only inside `failure`; `holdWriter.failed` wants a status of its own, 400 up (`fallback.go`); `errorReply` wants a top-level `error` and no `choices` beside it (`fallback.go:4353`); `saidAnything` counts a Text part as said (`ir.go:802`). So 200 → said → settle finds no envelope → the pitch goes out as the answer, and the account never rests.

**The fix**: the reporter's `failWords`, per provider, off by default — nothing is read of a reply a user hasn't opted in for, the same words in a real answer failing it being that user's own to weigh, which is why there is no global word list here (the words `creditWords`/`usedUpWords` know run on error bodies only, where a misread costs one ask again; a pitch-say on replies would cost real answers — the reporter says the same), and no guessing of what a pitch sounds like (#5089's rule: what a reply is read by is what the user wrote, never what magpie guessed of the language). A whole reply under a success status that says one of the words fails as its own words stand for (`streamStatus`, the way `errorReply` reads an error said under a 200): asked of the next member, the account rested as the vendor's own 429 would rest it, nothing new to the rest ladder; nobody left, the agent is told the failure and never handed the pitch. One word magpie can't read is dropped, not fatal. Differences from the reference patch, both ways down to less: the words are matched on the decoded body as `wordsOf` has it (all four reply shapes at once), with `pitchOf` lifting what the vendor actually said for the failure message, so no per-shape reader of replies is added; `newHoldWriter` keeps its signature — the words ride on the `holdWriter` as a field set where the try begins, so the mechanical change is two call sites, not fourteen; and a save of the editor's, whose form doesn't carry the words, keeps what was stored (set in providers.json by hand; #1604's kind of field). The reply as it streams is not read — the reporter's A1/A2 choice, which I am not making for you: a pitch already out stays out, the rest keeps the next request away, and holding past the first content would be the configured provider's TTFT to spend. #3967 (an upstream billing wrongly with a 200 and no usage) is the same family on another face — that one is the meter, this one is the words; neither reads what the other does, and neither depends on the other.

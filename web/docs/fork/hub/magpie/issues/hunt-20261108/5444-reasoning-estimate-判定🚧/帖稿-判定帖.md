**Where the design line sits**: the in-flight number is an estimate of what the request carried when it was sent — sealed reasoning is ciphertext, folded by its length because there is nothing else to read. Where a plugin drops items on the way out, the drop happens per outbound attempt, after the estimate; the calibrated, vendor-counted record is what persists. Making the in-flight estimate follow the cleaned body instead would mean re-reading it per attempt (per provider, per protocol translation) — a design change, not a wrong number to fix, and the persisted record already lands on the vendor's count.

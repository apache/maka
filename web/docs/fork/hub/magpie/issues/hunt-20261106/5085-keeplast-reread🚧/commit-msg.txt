provider: a careful re-read reads a saved Claude account too (#5079)

A saved Claude account's usage is read only when its card is refreshed,
when the refresh of every allowance reaches it, when the switch off a
spent one needs it, or when the keep-alive runs (claudeWindows, #1630);
any other reading of it is what was kept. A careful provider's re-read
was none of these: readCareful asked ReadUsageNow, which dropped the
caches and asked again, and claudeWindows handed the kept reading back,
so the ask never became a read. An account held as unknown stayed held,
asked again at most carefulRetry later, answered with the same kept
reading, held again: #5085's saved account under its cap was kept out
for good, and #5079's pool answered 429 at every request after a
restart until a card was refreshed by hand.

ReadUsageNow now records the ask (readAsked, by agent/user), and
claudeWindows answers it for a saved account: the ask is the reach into
it that its card's refresh, the refresh of every allowance, the switch
and the keep-alive already were. The ask is taken at the account's next
sight, read or kept, and the careful re-read asks again at most
carefulRetry later, so what spaces the runs is what already capped
them: the 30-second floor (claudeAskFloor) and a failed read's wait. An
account whose /usage keeps failing is held as before — unknown is not
spent (plugins#349) — without a Claude Code started on every request
that holds it.

With the read real, careful judges the account by the reading's real
age: one read back in time, the saved account under its cap takes the
request (#5085). TestCarefulRereadsASavedClaudeAccount walks it at the
gateway, in the reporter's shape: two Claude accounts, each capped at
90% of its five hours, the first past its cap, the saved one at 30%
with nothing read since magpie started, careful on — red on main (429,
every account held or unread, the saved account's /usage never run),
green here. TestReadUsageNowReadsASavedClaudeAccount pins the ask at
the provider seam: ReadUsageNow of a saved account runs its /usage, and
routing reads it fresh after.

Fixes #5079, the root; with the read real, #5085's saved account is no

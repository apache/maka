fix(redact): an error's words for a missing module are read before the quote, not the whole body before it (#4766)

What changed

missingModuleAt said a quoted name is a module an error could not load by
lowering everything before the quote. A session's body holds a place a known
value turns up at for each turn it has been in the history, and knownSpans
asks packageAt at each of them, so every such place paid the body's whole
length again — a 555 KB turn held 1m54s in the masking, past the unrouted
budget, and was answered 504 (#4766). The words stand right before the
quote; what is read of what is before them is the last 64 bytes of it now,
as assignedSecret reads the 64 before the value it judges. A letter a lower
case folds wider than in those bytes reads the whole text before the quote,
as before. Nothing of what is masked changes.

**What magpie does with that 429 (and how it relates to quota rest)**

- In the plugin: `USER_REQUEST_RATE_EXCEEDED` is one of the three throttles (with `CREDIT_CONSUMPTION_RATE_EXCEEDED`, `SERVICE_REQUEST_RATE_EXCEEDED`) that 0.1.32+ rides out the way the IDE's `QClientRetryStrategy` does — up to 3 tries, waiting 1s/2s or what Kiro says; a wait past 20s goes back to magpie at once with `Retry-After` (30s when Kiro says none). A daily/monthly usage limit and `INSUFFICIENT_MODEL_CAPACITY` are not retried.
- In magpie core: a member's 429-with-Retry-After is filed under busy (a moment's rate limit) — that account rests for the Retry-After, the next member is asked, and members back by then are asked again in turn within the request's patience; when the caller hangs up the request ends 499 with the underlying 429s kept in `tries`. That is the exact shape of both traces in this issue (4 tries, all 429, 499 after ~40s). It is a different class from the exhausted-quota rest (a used-up daily/monthly limit rests the account for its window); `USER_REQUEST_RATE_EXCEEDED` is deliberately not that. It is also the opposite side of the boundary from #5692/#3967, where an upstream answers 200 with billing text and gets recorded as success — here the failure is explicit, classified, and rested/rotated as designed.

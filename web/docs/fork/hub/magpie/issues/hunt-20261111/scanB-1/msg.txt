provider, gateway, gui: a provider's speech and embedding models sit in its editor, and /v1/models says what a kind model is for

Fish-under-sea (#5418) had nowhere to put a provider's whisper-1, tts-1
and embedding models: its editor lists its image models (a hidden-drawers
pick) and its video ones, and the models made for another API sat in no
list; nor did /v1/models say what a model is for, so an SDK reading a
flat list guessed from ids.

- provider: HiddenSpeech and HiddenEmbeddings, kept and carried as
  HiddenDrawers and HiddenVideomakers are, read by SpeechHidden and
  EmbeddingHidden. A plugin's models its capabilities.output says are
  made for another API — audio alone a TTS, nothing said an embedding,
  the speechless ones #3279 drops from the chat lists — are kept apart
  (PluginServing), told by what the plugin declares, not the id.
- gateway: ServingOf is the models a provider serves of an API that is
  no chat: a fetched list's, another magpie's under an id that says so,
  a preset plan's, a plugin's declared ones. servesKind reads it, and
  /v1/models' ?kind= takes embedding, rerank, speech and transcription,
  each row marked with its kind (servingObjects), less those unticked in
  their provider's editor, as OfferedDrawers are of the image ones. A
  request that names a hidden one still serves.
- gui: the editor lists its speech models and its embedding and rerank
  ones apart (speechIds, embedIds), each ticked, a click unticking it
  into hiddenSpeech / hiddenEmbeddings, as its image and video ones are;
  and the core scripts' budget says why it grew.

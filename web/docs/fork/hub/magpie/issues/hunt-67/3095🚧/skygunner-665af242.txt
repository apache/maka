    test(provider): keep hold assertions independent of cache expiry
    
    TestHoldReadsMigrationsOnce and three count-sensitive siblings require
    one held value, but the production one-second TTL can expire between
    adjacent statements on a loaded runner. Pin the TTL to an hour within
    those tests and restore it afterwards, using the existing in-file pattern.
    The simple build-count fixture also starts with no key from a prior run.
    Production expiry and invalidation rules remain unchanged.
    
    Adapt ggbdpq/magpie de90e3eaf61693d5432583f38e2d183aecb335f1
    and cover TestHoldBuildsOnce, TestHoldReadsSettingsOnce and
    TestHoldBuildsProvidersOnce too. A temporary 1.2s pause between held reads
    fails all four original tests; with the fix the same pause passes three
    times under -race. Without the pause, all four pass ten times under -race,
    and the simple build-count test passes twenty isolated repetitions.
    No artificial delay is committed.
    
    Fixes yetone/magpie#3095
    Co-authored-by: ggbdpq <ggbdpq@gmail.com>
